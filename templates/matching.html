How scores are built

  • Skills ({{ (weights.skills*100)|round|int }}%) – required skills found in the resume
  • TF-IDF ({{ (weights.tfidf*100)|round|int }}%) – cosine similarity of preprocessed text
  • Keywords ({{ (weights.keywords*100)|round|int }}%) – job keywords present in the resume
  • Experience ({{ (weights.experience*100)|round|int }}%) – estimated years vs. requirement

Unused components are dropped and weights renormalised. Adjust weights in Settings.
